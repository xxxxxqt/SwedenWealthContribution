Wealth Distribution of Sweden

1995

In 1995, wealth was already highly concentrated. The top groups controlled most assets while the bottom half held only a small share. This is the baseline used to track how concentration evolves over time.

2000

Financial-asset appreciation during the late-1990s boom increased concentration. Gains were stronger among already asset-rich households. In this dataset, negative Bottom 50% values indicate net debt exceeding assets for that group.

2008

The global crisis disrupted balance sheets across the distribution. Lower-wealth households were more exposed to debt stress and weaker recovery. The negative range below zero represents debt, showing how liabilities can outweigh assets for the bottom half.

2020

During COVID-era volatility and policy shifts, asset prices rebounded quickly. Wealth concentration accelerated while broad-base ownership remained limited. The top continues to expand its share.

2023

By 2023, concentration is markedly higher than the baseline. The cumulative shift from 1995 is visible in the larger top-group footprint. Bottom 50% remains below zero in this series, which means the group's net position is still debt-heavy.
